# Enduring Republic Initiative
The United States of America constitution, better.

Among the oldest and best constitutions of the entire world, this document has been the basis the of the greatest society in human history. 250 years has exposed limitations, excesses and flaws. It is time to correct them.

Two branches exist, aiming for different paths to the same legal framework. One effects modifications through amendments. In theory, this would be the more practical resolution. The second creates a new constitution entirely. The country has become so fragmented that the idea of a constitutional convention may be more a realistic option.
