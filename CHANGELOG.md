# Changelog

Legal-text changes to `new/` and `amended/`, grouped by git tag. GitHub releases use the same milestones.

## [amendments-folded] - 2026-10-18

Every remaining amendment is now operative text in the articles. `new/amendments.md` is removed.

### new/

- Folded the 27th into Article I §6. No law varying the compensation of Senators and Representatives takes effect until an election of Representatives.
- Folded the 16th into Article I §8. Congress may tax incomes without apportionment among the States.
- Folded the 15th, 19th, 24th, and 26th into Article I §11. The vote is not denied for race, sex, failure to pay a tax, or age of eighteen and over.
- Folded the 23rd into Article II. The seat of government appoints electors for President and Vice President.
- Folded the remaining 20th into Articles I and II. The terms of the President and Vice President end at noon on January 20. If the President elect has died, the Vice President elect becomes President. If the President elect has failed to qualify, the Vice President elect acts until a President qualifies.
- Folded the 22nd into Article II. No person is elected President more than twice.
- Folded the 25th into Article II. On removal, death, or resignation, the Vice President becomes President. The sentence that the powers and duties devolve on the Vice President is removed.
- Folded the 11th into Article III §2. The judicial power does not extend to a suit against a State by citizens of another State or of a foreign State.
- Folded the 13th into Article IV §5. Neither slavery nor involuntary servitude shall exist, except as punishment for a crime.
- Folded the first ten amendments in as Article VII.
- Folded the 14th in as Article VIII: citizenship, the apportionment penalty, disqualification, the public debt, and enforcement.

### amended/

- No legal text changed.

## [spent-text-removed] - 2026-09-07

Spent and superseded clauses removed from new/. The articles now state current operative rules where a later amendment had already replaced the 1787 text. Remaining amendments are still a separate list, to be folded in later.

### new/

- Removed the three-fifths clause and the 1788 first-House seat list; apportion Representatives by the whole number of persons in each State.
- Removed the slave-trade clause (Art. I §9).
- Removed the 1808 lock on amending Art. I §9 (Art. V).
- Removed the fugitive slave clause (Art. IV §2).
- Replaced the original two-vote electoral procedure with distinct President and Vice-President ballots (Art. II).
- Replaced legislative election of Senators with popular election and popular vacancy filling (Art. I §3).
- Removed the `Places of chusing Senators` exception from the Elections Clause (Art. I §4).
- Replaced the first-Monday-in-December meeting with noon on January 3 (Art. I §4).
- Removed Article VII and the 1787 attestation, interlineations, and signatures.
- Removed the 1787 citizen grandfather from presidential eligibility; the natural-born requirement remains.
- Removed Amendments XVIII and XXI.
- Removed Amendment XII (procedure now in Article II); retargeted Amendment XXIII to the second Article.
- Removed Amendment XVII (procedure now in Article I).
- Removed Amendment XX §2 (January 3 meeting now in Article I) and spent XX §§5–6.
- Struck `excluding Indians not taxed` from Amendment XIV §2.
- Removed the Truman grandfather and the seven-year ratification sunset from Amendment XXII.
- Updated Amendment XIV §2 so the House-seat penalty is measured against citizens eighteen years of age, not male inhabitants twenty-one; the penalty itself remains.
- Removed leftover closing markdown fences.

### amended/

- No legal text changed.
